title: Ark of the Covenant
reference: Exodus 25:10-22
dimensions: 2½ × 1½ × 1½ cubits · 1.125 × 0.675 × 0.675 m
---

The Ark was made of acacia wood and overlaid inside and out with pure gold. Gold rings held its permanent carrying poles. A pure-gold mercy seat with two facing cherubim covered the Ark.
