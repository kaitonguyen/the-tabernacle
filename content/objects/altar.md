id: altar
title: Bronze altar of burnt offering
reference: Exodus 27:1-8
dimensions: 5 × 5 × 3 cubits · 2.25 × 2.25 × 1.35 m
---

The hollow altar was made from acacia boards overlaid with bronze. A horn rose from each corner. Its bronze grating, rings, poles, and utensils allowed it to be used and carried through the wilderness.
